About Punjab

Punjab is a culturally rich region known for its vibrant traditions, strong community values, and welcoming hospitality. The lifestyle of Punjab reflects celebration, unity, and deep respect for heritage.

One of the most important aspects of Punjabi culture is its connection to agriculture. The land is fertile and known for producing wheat, rice, and mustard crops. This strong farming background directly influences the cuisine and daily life of people.

Punjabi food is not just about eating; it is about sharing experiences. Meals are prepared with love and are often enjoyed together with family and guests. This creates a strong emotional and cultural bond.

Music and dance such as Bhangra and Gidda also play an important role in expressing joy and celebrating life. These traditions make Punjab a unique and lively culture.

Traditional Recipe

Sarson da Saag

Ingredients:

  • Mustard greens (sarson)
  • Spinach leaves
  • Ginger & garlic
  • Green chilies
  • Butter (makhan)
  • Salt & spices
  • Corn flour (optional thickening)

Method:

Boil and mash the greens until soft. Cook slowly with butter, garlic, ginger, and spices on low heat. Slow cooking enhances flavour and creates the traditional Punjabi taste.

Serve hot with makki di roti and extra butter for authentic flavour.
