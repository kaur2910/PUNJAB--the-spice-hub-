Traditional Recipe

Sarson da Saag
Ingredients (Detailed & Cultural Importance):
  • Mustard greens – main base, rich earthy taste
  • Spinach – softens texture & balances bitterness
  • Ginger – warmth & digestion
  • Garlic – deep aroma
  • Green chilies – light spice
  • Butter – authentic Punjabi richness
  • Spices – salt, turmeric, chili powder
  • Corn flour – optional thickening
Method:

Wash greens thoroughly and boil until soft. Blend into a thick paste. Cook slowly with butter, garlic, ginger, and spices. Slow cooking enhances flavour and gives authentic Punjabi richness.

Serve hot with makki di roti. This dish represents warmth, tradition, and Punjabi culture.
