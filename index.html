About Punjab

Punjab is known for its vibrant culture, rich traditions, and strong sense of community. Life in Punjab is filled with energy, celebration, and warmth. From colourful festivals to daily life, everything reflects happiness and togetherness.

Food plays a central role in Punjabi culture. It is not just about taste, but about sharing moments with family and friends. Meals are prepared with love, using fresh ingredients and traditional methods passed down through generations.

Popular Dishes

Sarson da Saag

Makki di Roti

Traditional Recipe

Sarson da Saag

Ingredients: Mustard greens, spinach, ginger, garlic, butter, spices.

Method: Wash and boil greens, blend into a paste, then cook slowly with butter and spices. This slow cooking enhances the flavour and gives an authentic Punjabi taste.

This dish represents warmth, comfort, and tradition. It is commonly enjoyed during winter and brings families together.
