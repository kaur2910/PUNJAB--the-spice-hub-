About Punjab

Punjab is one of the most culturally vibrant regions, known for its rich traditions, strong community values, and welcoming nature. The culture of Punjab reflects joy, celebration, and unity, which can be seen in its festivals, music, and daily life.

Agriculture plays a central role in Punjab, making it one of the most fertile regions. The connection between land and lifestyle is deeply rooted, influencing food habits and traditions.

Punjabi cuisine is known for its bold flavours and generous use of butter, spices, and fresh ingredients. Food is not just a necessity but a way to bring people together and express hospitality.

Traditional dances like Bhangra and Gidda show the energy and happiness of Punjabi culture, making it lively and unique.

Traditional Recipe

Sarson da Saag

Sarson da Saag is a classic Punjabi dish that represents warmth, comfort, and tradition. It is commonly prepared during winter and served with makki di roti and fresh butter.

Ingredients:

  • Mustard greens (sarson)
  • Spinach
  • Ginger and garlic
  • Green chilies
  • Butter (makhan)
  • Salt and spices

Method:

Wash and boil the greens until soft. Mash them thoroughly to create a smooth texture. Cook the mixture slowly with butter, ginger, garlic, and spices. Slow cooking enhances the flavour and gives it an authentic Punjabi taste.

Serve hot with makki di roti and a dollop of butter. This dish represents the essence of Punjabi culture and hospitality.
