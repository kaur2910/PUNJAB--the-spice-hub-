About Punjab

Punjab is a culturally rich region known for its vibrant traditions, hospitality, and strong sense of community. Life in Punjab is full of celebration, music, dance, and togetherness.

Agriculture plays a major role in Punjab’s identity, making it one of the most fertile regions. The food culture is deeply connected to farming traditions and seasonal ingredients.

Punjabi meals are shared with love and respect, reflecting unity and family bonding. Cultural expressions like Bhangra and Gidda show joy and energy in everyday life.

Traditional Recipe

Sarson da Saag

Ingredients:

  • Mustard greens
  • Spinach
  • Garlic & ginger
  • Green chilies
  • Butter
  • Spices

Method:

Boil greens, mash them, and cook slowly with spices and butter. Slow cooking enhances flavour and gives authentic Punjabi taste.
