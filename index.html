About Punjab

Punjab is a land of vibrant culture, energetic festivals, and heartwarming hospitality. The cuisine reflects the agricultural richness of the region, combining bold spices, fresh ingredients, and traditional cooking techniques. Food is not just nourishment in Punjab, but a celebration of life, bringing families and communities together.

Popular Dishes

Punjabi dishes are known for their richness, texture, and comforting taste. These meals represent both tradition and emotional connection, often prepared during gatherings and special occasions.

Traditional Recipe

This section provides a deeper understanding of Punjabi cooking by explaining a traditional recipe. It highlights both the cultural importance and the preparation process, making the website more informative and engaging.

Sarson da Saag
Ingredients:
  • Fresh mustard greens (sarson)
  • Spinach leaves
  • Ginger and garlic paste
  • Green chilies
  • Makhan (butter)
  • Basic spices (salt, turmeric, red chili)
Detailed Method:

Begin by thoroughly washing the greens to remove any impurities. Boil them until soft and tender, then blend them into a coarse paste. Heat butter in a pan and sauté ginger, garlic, and green chilies until aromatic. Add the blended mixture and cook slowly on low heat, allowing the flavours to deepen. Stir occasionally to maintain texture. This slow cooking process is essential to achieve the authentic taste.

Serve hot with makki di roti and a generous amount of butter. This dish represents comfort, tradition, and the essence of Punjabi cuisine.
