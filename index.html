About Punjab

Punjab is a region known for its rich cultural heritage, energetic lifestyle, and deeply rooted traditions. Food plays a central role in Punjabi identity, reflecting warmth, hospitality, and bold flavours. Each dish tells a story of history, agriculture, and community, making Punjabi cuisine one of the most loved across the world.

Popular Dishes

Punjabi cuisine is famous for its comforting and flavourful dishes that bring people together. These traditional meals are not just food, but an experience of culture and tradition.

Sarson da Saag

A traditional winter dish made from mustard greens, slow-cooked with spices to create a rich and earthy flavour.

Makki di Roti

A classic Punjabi bread made from maize flour, perfectly complementing sarson da saag with its rustic taste.

Sarson da Saag Recipe

Ingredients:
  • Mustard greens (sarson)
  • Spinach
  • Ginger and garlic
  • Green chilies
  • Butter (makhan)
  • Spices (salt, turmeric)
Method:

Wash and boil the greens until soft. Blend them into a thick paste. In a pan, heat butter and sauté ginger, garlic, and chilies. Add the blended greens and cook on low heat for 20–30 minutes. Serve hot with makki di roti and a dollop of butter.
