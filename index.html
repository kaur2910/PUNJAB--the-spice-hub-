About Punjab

Punjab is a land of vibrant energy, deep traditions, and rich cultural heritage. It is known for its warm-hearted people, colourful festivals, and a lifestyle full of joy and celebration.

The identity of Punjab is built on unity, strength, and hospitality. Guests are treated like family, and every moment is celebrated with music, dance, and food. The famous Bhangra and Gidda dances represent happiness and community spirit.

Food is at the heart of Punjabi culture. It is not just nourishment but an expression of love and togetherness. Traditional dishes are prepared using fresh ingredients, slow cooking methods, and recipes passed down through generations. Every meal tells a story of culture, farming life, and family bonds.

Punjab’s fertile land, known as the “Granary of India,” produces rich crops that inspire its cuisine. From mustard fields to wheat farms, everything contributes to the authentic flavours that define Punjabi food.

Traditional Recipe

Sarson da Saag
Ingredients:
  • Mustard greens (Sarson) – main base of the dish
  • Spinach – balances bitterness and improves texture
  • Ginger – adds warmth and aroma
  • Garlic – enhances flavour depth
  • Green chilies – adds light spice
  • Butter (Makhan) – gives richness and authenticity
  • Salt, turmeric, chilli powder – essential seasoning
  • Optional corn flour – thickens the mixture
Method:

Wash greens thoroughly and boil until soft. Mash or blend them into a thick paste. In a pan, heat butter and sauté ginger, garlic, and chilies. Add the mixture and cook slowly on low heat. This slow cooking develops deep traditional Punjabi flavour.

Serve hot with makki di roti and extra butter on top for authentic taste.
