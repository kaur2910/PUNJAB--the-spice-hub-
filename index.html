About Punjab

Punjab is a land of vibrant traditions, rich heritage, and strong cultural identity. It is known for its lively festivals, energetic music, and welcoming people. The region reflects a lifestyle full of joy, unity, and celebration, where traditions are passed from generation to generation.

The food of Punjab plays a major role in its identity. It represents warmth, comfort, and togetherness. Meals are often prepared with fresh ingredients and rich spices, making them both flavourful and meaningful. Punjabi cuisine is not just about eating—it is about sharing experiences, creating memories, and expressing hospitality.

Traditional Recipe

This section provides a detailed insight into Punjabi cooking methods, highlighting both ingredients and cultural significance.

Sarson da Saag
Ingredients:
  • Mustard greens
  • Spinach
  • Ginger & garlic
  • Green chilies
  • Butter
  • Basic spices
Method:

Wash and boil greens until soft. Blend into a paste. Cook with butter, ginger, and spices on low heat for rich flavour. Serve hot with makki di roti.

This dish reflects Punjabi tradition, warmth, and the importance of homemade food.
